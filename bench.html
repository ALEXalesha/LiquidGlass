Drags one panel across the wallpaper under each configuration and counts frames. Everything here is about one question: what does a moving panel cost, and which part of it. Keep this tab in front and do not cover the window — a tab that is not being composited parks requestAnimationFrame, and then every number comes back as a dash rather than as an invented figure.

backdrop-filter with blur() and saturate() is handed to the gpu. The same property holding a url() filter with feImage and feDisplacementMap inside is not, and is rasterised on the cpu for the whole filter region, every frame the backdrop underneath changes. That is the entire reason a drag is expensive, and it is why the last row — no svg filter at all — is the ceiling nothing here can beat.
