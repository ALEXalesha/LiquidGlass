Liquid Glass — invariants

Not examples, properties. Every line below is a statement that has to hold for every input, checked against thousands of random ones. The pixel maths is pure, so it can be hammered directly; the bake queue and the element lifecycle are state machines, so they get random action sequences and are checked once they settle. A failure prints the case that broke it, and the seed reproduces the whole run.
